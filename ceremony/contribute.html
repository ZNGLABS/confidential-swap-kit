Contribute to the trusted setup

Confidential Swap Kit · phase 2 · runs entirely in this tab, nothing to install

What you are about to do. You will add your own randomness to the proving key of a zero-knowledge circuit, and then destroy it. As long as one single participant genuinely destroys theirs, nobody can ever forge a proof against this circuit — not us, not the other participants, not a future attacker who compromises everyone else.

You do not have to trust anyone here, including us. Your own honesty is what protects the system. That is the entire point of a trusted setup ceremony, and it is why it is reasonable to ask a stranger to do it.

Why this is being asked. Our phase-2 setup today has exactly one contribution, and it is labelled ceremonie de TEST. One machine knows the randomness. We say so in our README rather than waiting to be found out — and this page is how we fix it.

You can verify that claim yourself before contributing: snarkjs zkey verify lists every contribution in the key. The commands are in the repository.

1 · Check nobody else is contributing right now

A phase-2 contribution is sequential: two people starting from the same key at the same time produce two branches, and only one can be kept. Before you start, leave a comment on issue #1 saying you are taking a turn. It is a queue of one, held by a human. We are not going to pretend this is more automated than it is.

2 · Your randomness

Move your mouse around and type on your keyboard. We mix what you produce with your browser's cryptographic random generator. Both go in; either one alone would be enough for the setup to be sound.

What this page does, precisely


It downloads the current proving key (about 30 MB, in 8 parts) from our
public storage, runs snarkjs.zKey.contribute in this tab, and
hands you back the result. Your randomness is used in this tab and never leaves it: there is
no upload of anything you typed, and no analytics on this page. The whole computation takes
around 16 seconds on a normal laptop — the download is the slow part.
